[h1]What ZEIC does[/h1]
A tool to quickly decorate and populate interiors for your missions in either Zeus or Eden.

[h3]Interior Fill[/h3]
[list]
[*] Furnishes a building, or a share of the buildings within a radius, from the templates of a category (Military, Civilian, CBRN, and any category a mod or mission adds).
[*] Simple objects (fastest), normal objects, or objects editable in Zeus.
[*] Shortcuts, held while placing: Alt repeats the last fill, Ctrl fills Military, Shift Civilian, Ctrl+Shift CBRN.
[/list]

[h3]Garrison Building[/h3]
[list]
[*] Fills a building, or a share of the buildings within a radius, with infantry of any faction.
[*] Find the faction by side and search; recently used factions are listed on top.
[*] Hold Alt while placing to garrison again with the last settings.
[/list]

[h3]Fill Table / Shelf[/h3]
[list]
[*] Puts small objects on a table, shelf or rack (food, medical, office, tools, electric, CBRN cleaning or random).
[/list]

[h3]Object Switch[/h3]
[list]
[*] Swaps military objects within a radius for another camouflage set (e.g. green sandbags to brown ones), including CUP and SOG sets when those mods are loaded.
[/list]

[h3]For template authors[/h3]
[list]
[*] Save Building Scheme writes a template ready to paste into a mission's description.ext.
[*] List Buildings and Mark Positions help finding buildings that need templates.
[/list]

[h1]Technical Details[/h1]
[list]
[*] Standalone, client side, creates no dependency in the mission file.
[*] Built and signed with HEMTT; each update produces a new bikey.
[/list]
